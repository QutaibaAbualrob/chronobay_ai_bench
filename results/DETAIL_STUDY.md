## What this study cannot show

- **Dial colour and case size.** The prompt never asked for them and the answer key has no such columns.
- **Real seller photos.** These photos came from the web. The app will send five labelled photos per watch; this study used one.
- **Run-to-run stability of the strong models.** One run each.
- **The answer key's own errors.** Attribute columns were checked less closely than references; section 8 lists the rows to recheck.
